- (decision by coach) Core pillars: (1) Unified API & multi-provider routing/failover, (2) Auth/tenancy/quotas/budgets, (3) Reliability & performance (retry, circuit-break, caching), (4) Observability/evals & FinOps, (5) Safety/compliance/guardrails, (6) Developer experience & governance
- (insight by coach) MVP should prioritize OpenAI-compatible proxy, provider adapters, virtual keys+quotas, logging/cost metering, and basic failover; defer autonomous optimization, full eval harness, and advanced privacy attestation
- (insight by coach) Key differentiators vs basic proxy: policy-driven routing (cost/latency/quality), semantic caching, schema-validated outputs, and built-in eval/replay loop from prod traffic
- (decision by coach) Design for provider-agnostic contract, config-as-code policies, and gateway as trust boundary (treat inputs/outputs as untrusted with redaction and validation)
