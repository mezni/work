# Core Features - BioViva Ecommerce

## Job-to-Be-Done (JTBD)
Users hire BioViva to:
- **Discover authentic bio products** they can trust for quality and origin
- **Access the health benefits** promised by genuine olive oil, honey, and herbal essences
- **Connect with the story** behind each product (farmer, region, harvesting method)
- **Find rare/specialty bio products** not available in conventional stores

## Essential Features

### 1. Product Authentication & Traceability
- **Batch/lot numbering** on every product
- **Origin mapping** - geographic source visualization
- **Certification badges** (organic, fair trade, etc.)
- **Harvest date** display for freshness tracking

### 2. Curated Collection Pages
- **Seasonal selections** - olive harvest, honey flow periods, herb harvesting
- **Product categories**: Olive Oil, Honey, Herbal Essentials
- **Quality grades** with taste profiles and usage recommendations

### 3. Producer Stories
- **Farmer profiles** with photos and methods
- **Harvest diaries** - seasonal updates from the source
- **Sustainability metrics** - land stewardship, bee health, water use

### 4. Purity Testing Results
- Lab reports accessible per product
- Adulteration testing status
- FFA (Free Fatty Acids) for olive oil, pollen analysis for honey

### 5. Community & Education
- **Usage guides** - cooking temperatures, dosage, pairing suggestions
- **Freshness alerts** - when products approach optimal use-by
- **Comparison tool** - side-by-side product analysis

### 6. Smart Reordering
- **Subscription model** for staple products (olive oil, honey)
- **Running low notifications** based on usage patterns
- **Automatic reorder** with certification renewal alerts

## Differentiators vs Conventional Ecommerce
- Every product has a **traceability story**
- **Seasonal availability** mirrors natural harvest cycles
- **Freshness-first** dating (not just expiration)
- **Producer direct** relationship (no middle-grade bulk reselling)
