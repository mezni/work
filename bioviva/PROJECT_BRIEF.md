# BioViva - Ecommerce Platform for Bio Products

## Project Brief
A bio-product ecommerce system connecting consumers with authentic olive oil, honey, and herbal essences through traceability, education, and community.

## Core Problem
Consumers lack trust and transparency when buying bio products online - uncertainty about authenticity, freshness, and origin undermines purchasing decisions.

## Solution
BioViva - a platform that makes every product traceable, every farmer visible, and every purchase confidence-inspiring through sensory-rich design and ecosystem-based architecture.

## Key Differentiators
1. **Trust Score** (0-100) calculated from certification, lab results, producer reputation
2. **Season-first catalog** mirroring natural harvest cycles
3. **Full sensory profile** on every product (aroma, flavor, texture)
4. **Direct producer stories** with photos and methods
5. **Freshness tracking** from harvest to consumption

## Target Audience
- Health-conscious consumers aged 25-55
- Culinary enthusiasts seeking quality ingredients
- People with dietary restrictions requiring authentic products
- Gift-givers wanting meaningful, story-rich presents

## Success Metrics
- Trust score visibility → 40% increase in conversion from product page
- Seasonal collections → 25% higher average order value
- Producer story engagement → 30% repeat purchase rate
- Freshness alerts → 15% reduction in expired product complaints
