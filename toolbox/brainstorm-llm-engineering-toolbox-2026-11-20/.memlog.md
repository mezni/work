- (insight by coach) The buried winners are the constraint-derived ones: a 60-minute CLI that scores a CSV and diffs against baseline, one-line call logging, and experiment cards as diffable files — these make the whole toolbox testable before any UI exists
- (insight by coach) The odd idea that matters most: the red-team agent whose job is beating the evaluator — it turns evals from a static score into an adversarial loop that stays honest
- (insight by coach) Three ideas are one idea in different hats: flight recorder, time-travel debugging, and one-click repro all serve replayability — ship one trace format that powers all three
- (insight by coach) The real tension is not model quality vs cost, it is speed vs rigor — the two-tier eval (smoke gate plus deep nightly) resolves it and should be the product's rhythm
- (direction by coach) Positioning: a local-first, CLI-first evidence layer for LLM engineering; eval-driven, git-native, model-agnostic
- (direction by coach) MVP sequence: call logging and trace format, then eval runner and regression gate, then versioned bundles and rollback, then golden-set curation
