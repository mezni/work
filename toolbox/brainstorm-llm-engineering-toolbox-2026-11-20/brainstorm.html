Insights

Buried winners (constraint-derived)
The constraint-derived ones: a 60-minute CLI that scores a CSV and diffs against baseline, one-line call logging, and experiment cards as diffable files — these make the whole toolbox testable before any UI exists.
Odd idea that matters most
The red-team agent whose job is beating the evaluator — it turns evals from a static score into an adversarial loop that stays honest.
Three ideas, one idea
Three ideas are one idea in different hats: flight recorder, time-travel debugging, and one-click repro all serve replayability — ship one trace format that powers all three.
The real tension
The real tension is not model quality vs cost, it is speed vs rigor — the two-tier eval (smoke gate plus deep nightly) resolves it and should be the product's rhythm.

Positioning

A local-first, CLI-first evidence layer for LLM engineering; eval-driven, git-native, model-agnostic.

MVP sequence

  1. Call logging and trace format
  2. Then eval runner and regression gate
  3. Then versioned bundles and rollback
  4. Then golden-set curation
